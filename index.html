📘 Teacher guide — show instructions
✋ Left-drag: copy from the cutting board to any comparison or addition row. Elsewhere, dragging moves a block. Drop it onto another block to combine.   🖱️ Right-click: on the board choose Enter fraction… or Cut into 2, 3, 4, 6 or 8. Any block also offers ✨ Simplify with a worked solution.   👆 Double-click: simplify the fraction (e.g. 2/4 → 1/2).
📱 On phones and tablets: press and hold a block to open Cut / Simplify, or hold an empty cutting-board area to enter a fraction. Drag blocks with a finger; double-tap a block to simplify it. Tap a block, then tap an empty row to move it.
